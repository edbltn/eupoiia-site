This table counts only votes cast in the Daily 10 for 2026-11-20, including unfinished sets and shared-seed sets for that date. It excludes Random votes and Daily 10 sets from other dates. Signing in attaches eligible votes from the current tab to the account.

For each counted vote k, A is the left-hand contender and B the right-hand contender. Set yk to 1 for a pick of A, 0 for a pick of B, and ½ for a draw. Use the contenders' current Elo ratings to calculate A's expected score:

E_k = 1 / (1 + 10^((R_B,k − R_A,k) / 400))

The vote score is one minus its squared difference from that expectation:

s_k = 1 − (y_k − E_k)²

The Taste score is the mean of those vote scores multiplied by 100, where n is the number of votes counted:

Taste = (100 / n) × sum of s_k

The score ranges from 0 to 100. Higher scores mean the choices are closer to the crowd's current expectations. Equal scores are ordered by more votes first, then name. Votes are saved; the score is recalculated using current ratings whenever it is shown. It can change as other people vote, even after a Daily 10 is finished.
