Eupoiia Index

eu·poi·ï·a /juː ˈpɔɪ i ə/ n.

from Ancient Greek εὐποιΐα (eupoiḯa), from εὖ (eû, “well”) + ποιέω (poiéō, “to do, to make”) + -ία (abstract-noun suffix).

  1. Beneficence; the disposition or practice of doing good to others.
  2. A good deed; a benefaction.

Attested in: Hebrews 13:16 (“τῆς δὲ εὐποιΐας καὶ κοινωνίας μὴ ἐπιλανθάνεσθε”); cf. Liddell–Scott–Jones, s.v. εὐποιΐα.

Foresight score: points for picks the crowd underrated when you made them, and later came around to. The exact rule follows.

The Daily 10 table counts only Daily 10 votes, from every date, including unfinished sets and shared-seed sets. All time counts every vote: Random votes and Daily 10 votes from every date. Someone who isn't signed in is listed under a generated name, and signing in moves the current tab's eligible votes to their account.

For each vote, p is the chance Elo gave your pick when you voted and q is the chance it gives now, at the current ratings. Both leave out the push your own votes gave the two ratings, and both are held between 0.02 and 0.98. The vote earns, in bits:

b = log₂(q / p)

A pick at 20% that now stands at 80% earns 2 bits; a favorite that stays where it was earns 0; a pick the crowd moved away from loses bits. A draw counts as half a vote for each side. Every vote counts at once, from any date, without waiting. Your bits are averaged with a weight of 10 votes at 0, so a handful of votes counts for little, and the average b̄ is put on a scale from 0 to 100:

Foresight = 100 / (1 + 2^(−12 b̄))

50 means as good as the crowd; above 50, the crowd came round to your picks. Equal scores are ordered by more votes first, then name. A dash means no vote yet. Votes are saved; the score is recalculated whenever it is shown and can change as other people vote.
