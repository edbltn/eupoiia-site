Elvis Aaron Presley (January 8, 1935 – August 16, 1977) was an American singer and actor. Referred to as the "King of Rock and Roll", he is widely regarded as one of the most culturally significant figures of the 20th century. Presley's energetic and sexually provocative performance style, combined with a mixture of influences across color lines during a transformative era in race relations, brought both great success and initial controversy.

Molière

French playwright and actor (1622–1673)

Read moreCollapseWikipedia

Jean-Baptiste Poquelin (French pronunciation: [ʒɑ̃ batist pɔklɛ̃]; 15 January 1622 (baptised) – 17 February 1673), known by his stage name Molière (UK: , US: ; French: [mɔljɛʁ] ), was a French playwright, actor, and poet, widely regarded as one of the great writers in the French language and world literature. His extant works include comedies, farces, tragicomedies, comédie-ballets, and more. His plays have been translated into every major living language and are performed at the Comédie-Française more often than those of any other playwright today. His influence is such that the French language is often referred to as the "language of Molière".

French First Republic

republic governing France, 1792–1804

Read moreCollapseWikipedia

In the history of France, the French Republic (French: République française), retroactively referred to as the First Republic (French: Première République) and sometimes referred to in historiography as Revolutionary France, was founded on 21 September 1792 during the French Revolution. The First Republic lasted until the declaration of the First Empire on 18 May 1804 under Napoléon Bonaparte, although the form of government changed several times.

Each card represents two people or groups, living or dead, from any period: nations, empires, companies, organizations, movements, dynasties. Pick the one you think has done more for the common good.

Swipe the card left or right, or use the buttons. Swipe up to skip. On a keyboard, the left and right arrows pick, = or space is a draw, and the down arrow skips.

Every pick moves both ratings by standard Elo (K = 32), so beating a highly rated contender counts for more than beating an unknown one. A draw counts like a draw in chess. Everyone starts at 1500.

The contenders come from Wikidata, chosen by how many Wikipedia language editions and sister sites cover them. That makes them recognizable, and it also inherits Wikipedia's biases toward the English-speaking, Western, recent and male. Coverage decides who is eligible, never anyone's rating. Names, descriptions and pictures come from Wikidata, Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons, free images only; each picture links to its file page for the author and license.
