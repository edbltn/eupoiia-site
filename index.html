Eupoiia Index

eu·poi·ï·a /juː ˈpɔɪ i ə/ n.

Etymology: from Ancient Greek εὐποιΐα (eupoiḯa), from εὖ (eû, “well”) + ποιέω (poiéō, “to do, to make”) + -ία (abstract-noun suffix).

  1. Beneficence; the disposition or practice of doing good to others.
  2. A good deed; a benefaction.

Attested in: Hebrews 13:16 (“τῆς δὲ εὐποιΐας καὶ κοινωνίας μὴ ἐπιλανθάνεσθε”); cf. Liddell–Scott–Jones, s.v. εὐποιΐα.

Islamic State of Afghanistan

government of Afghanistan (1992–2002)

Read moreCollapseWikipedia

The Islamic State of Afghanistan (Dari: دولت اسلامی افغانستان, Dawlat-i Islāmī-yi Afġānistān, Pashto: دا افغانستان اسلامی دولت, Da Afghanistan Islami Dowlat) was a unitary Islamic state that was established in April 1992 under the Peshawar Accord. Its power was limited due to the country's second civil war, which was won by the Taliban, who seized Kabul in September 1996. The Islamic state then transitioned to a government-in-exile and led the anti-Taliban Northern Alliance. It remained the internationally recognized government of Afghanistan at the United Nations until 2001, when the Transitional Islamic State of Afghanistan was created and an Afghan Interim Administration took control of Afghanistan with US and NATO assistance following the overthrow of the first Taliban government. The Transitional Islamic State was subsequently transformed into the Islamic Republic, which existed until the Taliban seized power again in 2021 following a prolonged insurgency.

Kazuo Ishiguro

British novelist (born 1954)

Read moreCollapseWikipedia

Sir Kazuo Ishiguro (Japanese: 石黒 一雄, Hepburn: Ishiguro Kazuo; ; born 8 November 1954) is a Japanese-British novelist, screenwriter, musician, and short-story writer. He is one of the most critically acclaimed contemporary fiction authors writing in English, having been awarded several major literary prizes, including the 2017 Nobel Prize in Literature. In its citation, the Swedish Academy described Ishiguro as a writer "who, in novels of great emotional force, has uncovered the abyss beneath our illusory sense of connection with the world".

Ku Klux Klan

American white supremacist hate group

Read moreCollapseWikipedia

The Ku Klux Klan (KKK), also known as "The Klan", is an American far-right hate group. It has emerged in three major waves throughout its history. The ideologies of various Klan offshoots have differed, but mostly share beliefs of White supremacy, anti-Black racism, and racial segregation. Its tactics have included intimidation, violence, and bombings to achieve specific aims such as suppressing black voters, weakening emerging civil rights movements, and maintaining a racial hierarchy with White Protestants at the top. The klan has operated through a decentralized network of multiple small, autonomous, and affiliated organizations since the 1950s–1960s civil rights era.
