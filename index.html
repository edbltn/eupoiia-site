Alfred Nobel

Alfred Bernhard Nobel ( noh-BEL; Swedish: [ˈǎlːfrɛd nʊˈbɛlː] ; 21 October 1833 – 10 December 1896) was a Swedish chemist, inventor, engineer, and businessman. Nobel is known for inventing dynamite, as well as having bequeathed his fortune to establish the Nobel Prizes. He worked on various important contributions and inventions to science, holding 355 patents during his life.

Haiti

country in the Caribbean Sea

Read moreCollapseWikipedia

Haiti, officially the Republic of Haiti, is a country in the Caribbean on the island of Hispaniola in the Caribbean Sea, east of Cuba and Jamaica and south of The Bahamas. It occupies the western side of the island, which it shares with the Dominican Republic. Haiti is the third largest country in the Caribbean by area, and with an estimated population of 11.4 million, it is the most populous Caribbean country. The capital and largest city is Port-au-Prince.

Fugger

Augsburg-based family of European bankers

Read moreCollapseWikipedia

The House of Fugger (German pronunciation: [ˈfʊɡɐ]) is a German family that was historically a prominent group of European bankers, members of the fifteenth- and sixteenth-century mercantile patriciate of Augsburg, international mercantile bankers, and venture capitalists. Alongside the Welser family, the Fugger family controlled much of the European economy in the sixteenth century and accumulated enormous wealth. The Fuggers held a near monopoly on the European copper market.

Palestine

political entity that enjoys incomplete recognition as a state, located in West Asia

Read moreCollapseWikipedia

Palestine, officially the State of Palestine, is a country in the Southern Levant region of West Asia. Situated between the Mediterranean Sea and Jordan River, it comprises the territories of the West Bank and the Gaza Strip, both of which are under occupation by Israel. Palestine shares the majority of its borders with Israel, while the West Bank borders Jordan to the east and the Gaza Strip borders Egypt to the southwest. The West Bank is divided between 165 Palestinian enclaves under partial civilian rule by the Palestinian Authority, and Area C, under full Israeli control. Palestine's population exceeds five million; its proclaimed capital is Jerusalem, while Ramallah serves as its de facto administrative center.

Salma Valgarma Hayek Pinault ( HY-ek, Spanish: [ˈsalma ˈxaʝek]; née Hayek Jiménez; born September 2, 1966) is a Mexican and American actress and film producer. Each card represents two people or groups, living or dead, from any period: nations, empires, companies, organizations, movements, dynasties. Pick the one you think has done more for the common good.

Swipe the card left or right, or use the buttons. Swipe up to skip. On a keyboard, the left and right arrows pick, = or space is a draw, and the down arrow skips.

Every pick moves both ratings by standard Elo (K = 32), so beating a highly rated contender counts for more than beating an unknown one. A draw counts like a draw in chess. Everyone starts at 1500.

The contenders come from Wikidata, chosen by how many Wikipedia language editions and sister sites cover them. That makes them recognizable, and it also inherits Wikipedia's biases toward the English-speaking, Western, recent and male. Coverage decides who is eligible, never anyone's rating. Names, descriptions and pictures come from Wikidata, Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons, free images only; each picture links to its file page for the author and license.
